Mampe has been pouring since 1831. That is nineteen years before the first
Berlin train station opened, and the bitter they hand you at the door is still
made to that first recipe.

The manufactory sits in the old brewhouse of a wheat beer brewery on Am
Tempelhofer Berg, in the middle of Kreuzberg's Bergmannkiez. It is a working
distillery, a liqueur factory and a schnapps shop at the same time. Their own
description of the room is hard to improve on: *an almost sacred ambience, and
the opportunity to breathe in the rich history of Berlin. More Berlin is not
possible.*

## Four ways in

**Little Liquor Stories** (€12, 45–60 minutes) is the short version, in German
— the history, the house, and a tasting at the end. There is a separate
**Small Schnapps Stories** at the same length and the same €12, run in English,
which is the one to book if your group would rather not translate as they go.

**Great Schnapps Stories** (€24, 90 minutes) is the full sitting. It opens with
a glass of Mampe Bitter Drops made to the 1831 recipe, then travels from the
founding of the brand to the present, walks you through the manufactory, covers
the basics of how schnapps is actually made, and pours **six** spirits. Everyone
on it also gets a one-off 10% discount in the shop that day, which is a
dangerous thing to hand someone who has just tasted six spirits.

**Gin Tasting with your own gin production** (€77, 2–2.5 hours) is the one
where you stop being an audience. You taste, then you distil, then you bottle
what you made and take it home.

There are also two *Schnapps Stories on the Sofa* boxes — vol. 1 at €49 and
vol. 2 at €99 — which are the tasting without the trip, posted to you.

## One thing to know before you book

Mampe are refreshingly direct about this: the tours are built for a crowd. In
their words, the experience *only really becomes fun when there are ten
participants or more*, and a date with fewer people on it may be cancelled.

Which is an odd thing to read as a solo visitor and very good news if you are
looking for something to do with a team. You are not squeezing a group into a
format built for couples. The format wants the group.

The scheduled tours run in German; English is available on request, and the
English short tour above is already on the books.

## Also there

The yard shop is open Monday to Friday, 10 to 18, if you only want the bottle.
Upstairs of all this they keep a small Mampe museum, and there is a virtual
walk-through of the location on their site if you would like to see the room
before you commit a team to it.

Their next open dates are on the [Twiggli calendar](/happening-today/). Each
format keeps its own slot: the English short tour on Wednesdays at 15:00 and
Fridays at 14:00, the German short one Wednesdays and Saturdays at 14:00, the
90-minute Great Schnapps Stories Wednesdays and Saturdays at 16:00, and the gin
seminar on Saturday evenings at 18:00.
