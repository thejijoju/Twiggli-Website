Most people who run a craft studio arrived at it from one craft. Celina Schmidt
arrived from about six, and none of them started in a studio.

She has a Bachelor of Engineering in audiovisual media and a Master of Arts in
filmmaking. She spent years in the art departments of film and television
productions as a graphic prop maker — which is a job that quietly demands you
be able to make almost anything, convincingly, by Thursday. Somewhere alongside
that she has been structuring and designing educational content for a wide
range of subjects and clients, and she still works as a travel coordinator,
leading international tours and coming back with ideas.

That is an unusual CV for a workshop teacher, and it shows up in the two places
it matters: the range of things she can teach, and how well the teaching is
put together.

## The studio

Studio Gestaltwandel opened in 2025, but not from nothing — it grew out of
several workshop formats that had already been running successfully for more
than two years. The new space on Koloniestraße simply gave them room to
multiply.

There are two rooms. A large, bright one for group workshops, and a dedicated
artisan's space for the finicky work — stained glass and tile-making, the
crafts that want their own bench and their own mess. It sits in the calm middle
of Soldiner Kiez, a few minutes from U8 Osloer Straße or S1/S25 Wollankstraße.

She also rents the space out to other makers, which is the kind of thing people
do when they remember needing a room themselves.

## Nine things you can make there

**Paper and print.** *Bookbinding* is the anchor: notebooks to use as a zine,
a journal or a sketchbook, and softcover books with an open spine
(€59, 3 hours) or the fuller notebook binding (€69, 3.5 hours). *Envelope
folding* needs no glue at all — paper, a few folds, and you have envelopes in
minutes. *Stamp carving* (€50, 2.5 hours) sends you home with your own little
rubber stamps.

**Glass, stone and metal.** *Stained glass soldering* teaches the traditional
Tiffany technique with copper foil and lead, and you leave with a flat artwork
or a three-dimensional object. *Mosaic tile making* (€89, 4.5 hours) turns
ceramic or glass into coasters or wall pieces. *Tile making* casts a coloured
tile from concrete. *Tin ornament crafting* is embossed tin paper, the way
vintage ornaments were made.

**Cloth and straw.** *Silk painting* is for anyone who has ever wanted to
design their own scarf. And *strawstar crafting* (€59, 2 hours) is the oldest
thing on the list — straw ornaments have been hanging in German windows and on
German Christmas trees for centuries, and they are much harder than they look.

No experience is needed for any of them. Sessions run in German and English.

## For a team

Every format also runs as a private group session on a date of your choosing —
up to twenty people in the studio, and fifty to a hundred if she brings it to
you. Two to four hours, so it fits an afternoon rather than eating a day.

It is a good fit for the kind of team event where half the room is quietly
dreading a team event. Nobody has to be good at this. You cut the glass, or
you fold the paper, or you bind the spine, and three hours later there is an
object that did not exist when you arrived.
