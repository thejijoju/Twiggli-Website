East Africa is where coffee comes from. Angelo's classes at Coffee & Bananas in
Prenzlauer Berg start exactly there: with the raw green bean, which smells of
nothing at all. What it becomes is decided over the next twenty minutes above
the flame — by ear, colour and smell, not by a timer.

## What happens in the class

Two hours, two different beans. You roast each one yourself, grind it fresh and
drink it — and only side by side does it become obvious how far apart two
roasts can land, with the same person at the same pot.

Grind size, water temperature, brewing method: those are the three dials that
decide the taste, and over these two hours you turn all three yourself.

Whatever is left goes home with you. That is the part nobody expects — you
leave with a bag of coffee you roasted, not with a memory of having done it.

The class runs in German and English, for six to fourteen people, and costs €59.

## Why East Africa

This is not a hobbyist importing beans. Angelo and his girlfriend, who runs
Coffee & Bananas with him, have coffee trees of their own in Kenya. That is why
there is so much talking in this class: how it is grown, what happens to the
cherry long before there is a bean, who earns along that chain and who does not.

You can take a roasting class and never learn any of it. Here it is half the
class.

## Coffee tasting, Saturdays

Alongside the roasting, a tasting runs on Saturdays from 11:00 to 12:20.
Nothing is roasted there — things are compared: East African coffees side by
side, brewed several ways, where one bean turns into two different drinks
depending on the grind and the water.

Tea tastings run separately.

To book a tasting, go straight to Angelo: **+49 176 765 064 56** (DM works too) or
